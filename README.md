# social-algebra

An attempt at an algebra of drives, of persons and of groups, composed
into an equation set for predicting and steering group outcomes. A
thinking strand with a simulator, not an app. See `CLAUDE.md` for the
brief and `STATUS.md` for the honest state.

**Closed with a write-up on 2026-11-01: `docs/write-up.md`.** Three
pre-registered experiments and a validated survey of 118 files found that
drives as pre-measured dispositions carry little of a group's outcome;
what a person did last time, plus coupling and structure, carries most of
it. The equation set (`models/`), the survey (`docs/prior-work/`), the
claim ledger (`docs/ledger.jsonl`) and the simulator (`sim/`) stand as
built.
