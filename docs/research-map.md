# Research map

## What we are trying to achieve

_Assembled from Aldred's words on 2026-10-29; update whenever he refines it, and date the change. Every batch report opens by restating it in one line._

An **algebra of drives**, of persons and of groups, composed into an
**equation set** with theorems, usable to **predict or steer outcomes** at
three scales — membership (who joins, stays, leaves, conforms), group
(cohesion, decision, split, efficiency) and crowd or society (adoption,
mobilisation, opinion) — **including positively** (better-functioning
groups), and **sensitive to group size, composition and definition**.
Existing societies and groups are working models: commonalities to abstract
from, and better-behaving groups to learn the factors from. Tested first on
a modelled population built independently of the equations, then on data.
"Drives as primitives" is the working hypothesis, tested by every dive, not
assumed.

Date: 2026-10-29

Purpose: the queue of research directions to run before any synthesis of the drive equation set.
Each direction is sized for one agent in one sitting: a survey, a deep-read, a cross-check, a dataset hunt or a model build.
Directions are surveyed in iterations; every finding must be validated with checked links before it can feed an equation or a model in `sim/`.

Status legend: **queued** (not started) / **surveying** (an agent is on it) / **validated** (findings second-passed with checked links) / **deep-read** (primary source read in full) / **modelled** (implemented and tested in `sim/`).

Kinds: survey, deep-read, cross-check, data, model.

Source: 651 raw directions generated from `docs/prior-work/01`–`42` (41 was still in validation when the map was built) plus new lenses. Near-duplicates were merged into **546 directions** (551 after D547–D551 for the modelled-population testbed; 560 after clusters O and P were added the same day from Aldred's target outcomes; 573 after Q social control and R sociology of knowledge; 582 after S comparative; 586 after T reflexivity and composition-vs-simulation, all his; 595 after V, the nine deep-reads v0 rests on). Every merged entry keeps the seeds and the `From` sources of all its parts and says what it absorbed. No distinct direction was dropped. IDs are stable: a new direction gets the next free number and is never renumbered into a cluster.
