# Brief: what the survey found, and three questions

_2026-10-29. One screen. The full argument is `docs/synthesis.md`._

## Found

1. Every piece exists somewhere: role semigroups and kinship groups (a real algebra, but of ties and fixed rules); agent–group–role and RBAC (the right sorts, but only membership operations); Prom Week and Versu (executable social moves, no laws); Singh's commitments and dynamic epistemic logic (directed acts that compose, but one relation each).
2. Statecraft, Simmel, family therapy and persuasion research name about 40 distinct moves between them; none says how moves combine.
3. Nobody puts acts that compose, over persons, roles and groups as separate sorts, with state and change, in one algebra. That is the open space (requirements R1–R11 in the synthesis).
4. Almost every formal description in the survey is from abstracts or textbook accounts, not read at source; whatever home is chosen, its primary texts get read next.
5. Six candidate homes are laid out with fit and cost; none is recommended.

## Three questions that decide the home

Defaults are the coordinator's (next-pattern), chosen as cheapest to extend; overrule freely.

- **Q1. Operands.** Default: persons, roles, groups only; classes and institutions enter later as kinds of group. Rules out, for now: events as operands (Luhmann), institutions as their own sort.
- **Q4. Quantities and affect.** Default: discrete, relational state first (who holds what role, who is committed to whom); scalars (affect, opinion, power) as a later layer. Rules out, for now: ACT-style affect arithmetic and opinion dynamics.
- **Q5. Dynamics.** Default: discrete turns, nondeterministic; claims are of the form "this state is reachable from that one by these acts"; rates and probabilities later, for fitting data. Rules out, for now: continuous time and statistical prediction.

The other seven questions (what a role is, dyad or triad, the other's view of me, which acts, observability, fit-first, choosing a home) wait until these three are answered.
