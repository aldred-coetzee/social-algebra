## 1. The outcome

**Definition 1.1 (outcome).** A group G is a finite set of n persons. Fix a horizon T ∈ ℕ (turns, §2). The outcome at T is the pair

  ω_T = (Π_T, L_T),  L_T ⊆ G,  Π_T a partition of G ∖ L_T into non-empty blocks B_1, …, B_k.

L_T is the **leaver set**; a block is a set of members still together at T.

**Predicates on ω_T.**
- holds(ω_T) ⇔ k = 1 and L_T = ∅.
- splits(ω_T) ⇔ k ≥ 2.
- who-leaves(ω_T) = L_T together with the block map β_T : G ∖ L_T → {1, …, k}.

holds and splits are not complementary: k = 1 with L_T ≠ ∅ is "holds with leavers" (attrition without schism). v0 is written against ω_T, not against any one instrument.

**Instruments that realise ω_T** (092 §2 and batch-2 report A6; none spans all three sizes as read):

|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | signed triad over time: Π_T = the two-colouring of the signed triad where it is balanced; L_T = members whose every tie has dissolved | Π, L | L4177 [read]; data D594 (L4286 ⚠ sa) |
| 3 (dyad atom) | divorce / dissolution as an exit event | L | 088, L3932 [read]; dyads only |
| 8–12 | observed fission of a named group (Walker & Hill, D593); faction partition by sign of the leading eigenvector of the tie matrix (Zachary, fitted offset) | Π | L3826 [read] (fit, not prediction) |

**The instrument per size is Aldred's choice.** v0 commits only to ω_T.

## 2. Sorts and state

The gating decision made turns nondeterministic; v0's laws are deterministic maps and nondeterminism is carried only by the inputs (the signs of §2.2) and by noise added in the testbed. That is a v0 simplification, not a result. Roles are not used in v0.

## 3. The three drives the evidence forces

Each drive is a term in the per-person update. Generic form (discrete turns):

  x_i(t+1) = x_i(t) + Δ¹_i(t) + Δ²_i(t) + Δ³_i(t).   (3.0)

**D1 — set point** (pull toward one's own baseline).

  Δ¹_i = −(1 − r_i)(x_i − p_i).   (3.1)

Derivation from Gottman's printed form [derived, shown]: a_i + r_i x_i = (1 − r_i) p_i + r_i x_i = x_i − (1 − r_i)(x_i − p_i) with p_i = a_i/(1 − r_i) (L0853 [read], L3919 [read]). The same form is Wankhede's −w_i(z_i − p_i) (L3993 [read]), Altafini's decay −x_i with p_i = 0 (L3856 [read]), the FJ anchor (L3994 [read]), and 081's fill–discharge cell dx/dt = r(1 − x) − s x = −(r + s)(x − r/(r + s)) (L1824 [derived], one line). It is the linear case of the Keramati–Gutkin drive D(H) = |p − x|^{n/m} at n/m = 2 (L0204 [read]), i.e. gradient descent on a quadratic drive. At crowd scale D1 is Kuran's integrity term N(1 − |x^i − y^i|) (L3965 [read]). Evidence that D1 carries most of the signal: the uninfluenced steady states are the strongest in-sample divorce correlates (F = 14.86, 18.40; 088 §2.4 [read]).

**D2 — gated affiliation** (pull toward others' state, only beyond a threshold).

  Δ²_i = Σ_{j ≠ i} I_ij(x_j, x_i),  with  I_ij = 0  whenever the incoming signal lies inside i's gate.   (3.2)

Ledger-backed forms by size:
- dyad: I_ij(x_j) = 0 for K⁻_i ≤ x_j ≤ K⁺_i; a bounded constant c⁺_i above K⁺_i; a bounded constant c⁻_i below K⁻_i — the O-jive, a function of the partner's level, not of the difference (L0857 [read]). The husband's K⁻ is the one coupling parameter with a direction in the divorce data (−7.71 divorced vs −5.36 stable, F(1,123) = 8.25; L3932 [read]; one of ten tests).
- team: I_ij = (K1/N) a_ij sin(x_j − x_i), unsigned a_ij ∈ [0,1] that itself opens or closes with heading similarity (L3871 [read]); K1 is the ratio of social coupling to goal strength (L3865 [read]).
- signed linear: I_ij = a_ij (x_j − x_i), a_ij of either sign (L3993 [read]; L3849 [read] as restated).
- crowd: the gate is the whole term: y_i(t+1) = 1 if r(t) ≥ θ_i, else 0 (L3960, L3959 [read]); Kuran's reputational utility R(y) with f, F increasing in the shares (L3957 [read], L3965 [read]).

A caution on what "coupling" means: 062's coupled drive D^i = Σ_j W_ij D_j (L0992 [derived]) couples **objectives**, not states; in the quadratic case its gradient with respect to x_i has no cross term, so it is not D2. v0 uses state coupling (3.2) and leaves objective coupling out.

**D3 — goal attraction** (pull toward a preferred option).

  Δ³_i = ω_i sin(g_i − x_i)  on S¹;  linear case ω_i (g_i − x_i).   (3.3)

Leonard's preference term with coefficient 1 (L3864 [read]); Couzin's |ω| as intransigence, uninformed members having ω_i ≈ 0 but a_ij > 0 (L3866 [read]). On a line, D3 and D1 have the same form with g_i in place of p_i; they are kept separate because g_i is a stated option (an input of the decision) and p_i is a measured baseline, and because Leonard's threshold (§4.3) depends on the angle between goals and on nothing in D1.

**Not forced.**
- *Status.* No read law bearing on ω_T has a status term. Leskovec's status rule f_status(τ) = sign(s(u,w) + s(w,v)) (L4196 [read]) is a predictor of the **sign of a directed tie**, a rival to balance for sgn(a_ij), not a term in the state update; it belongs to the sign input of §2.2, which v0 does not derive.
- *Threat.* Enters only as a **cost inside a gate**: the cost of arrest in Granovetter's threshold (L3959 [read]) and repression as a shift of Kuran's threshold function (L3976 [read]). No read law has threat as a state variable with its own dynamics.
- *A triad-consistency drive.* L3796 ⚠ sa, and interpretive; the product rule is a constraint on the relation graph that holds whatever the drives are (L4290 [derived]). It enters §4.2 as a constraint, not a drive.

**Why this is smaller than the seven-drive basis of 050.** 050's basis (affiliation, status, threat, seeking, care, mating, anger) is a modelling proposal from a concordance of questionnaire lists, labelled there as "a modelling judgement" (050 §4; its lines L0362/L2972–L2974 are `sa`). v0 keeps only drives that appear as a term in a law that was read and that bears on ω_T. D2 is 050's affiliation; D3 is the goal-pursuit part of seeking; D1 (homeostatic set point, L0204) has no counterpart in 050's list. Status, care, mating and anger have no read term; threat is a cost. v0 is a floor, not a claim that the others do not exist.

## 4. The update law per size

### 4.1 Dyad (the atom under the triad)

  x_1(t+1) = a_1 + r_1 x_1(t) + I_12(x_2(t)),  x_2(t+1) = a_2 + r_2 x_2(t) + I_21(x_1(t)),   (4.1)

### 4.2 Triad (n = 3)

Relation layer, continuous time as read (a discrete-turn version is Antal's CTD, L3828 [read], simulation only):

  dA/dt = A²,  i.e. da_ij/dt = Σ_k a_ik a_kj   (4.2a; L0898 [read])

with factions S = {k : ω_1k > 0}, T = {k : ω_1k < 0} for the leading eigenvector ω_1 when λ_1 > 0, λ_1 ≠ λ_2 and all ω_1k ≠ 0 (L3154 [read]). The authors call it a cartoon; the Zachary agreement used a fitted offset (L3826 [read]).

Static constraint on the sign pattern:

  sign(C) = Π_{(i,j) ∈ C} sgn(a_ij) for every closed path C; balanced iff every C is positive iff G two-colours into blocks positive within and negative across   (4.2b; L4170, L4171, L4177 [read])

⚠ **until D588**: L4298 (scope: any signed graph vs complete, open) and L4299 (the (−)(−) cell: enemy-of-my-enemy fails in the learned model on all three networks, open) are open contradictions touching split, so (4.2b) is not admissible as a total rule. What the data support is Davis's weak balance — forbid only the one-negative triangle (L4214, L4218 [read]; L4185 ⚠ sa for Davis himself), so (−)(−) is undefined, not + (L4293 [derived]).

State layer on the triad: ẋ = −L[A] x with L_jj = Σ_m |a_jm|, L_jk = −a_jk (L3849 [read] as restated). The dichotomy "bipartite consensus iff balanced, else all states → 0" is ⚠ **until D587** (L3835, L3837, L3840 open); what is read is Proskurnikov's Example 1, where a mutually hostile pair ignored by the third keeps x_1 = −x_2 = ξ (L3851 [read]) — so without strong connectivity an unbalanced triad need not decay.

v0 reading of ω_T at n = 3: Π_T is the two-colouring of sgn A(T) where it is balanced; where it is not (the one-negative triangle), the X² flow picks the side by sgn ω_1; a member whose both ties are negative and who is on the singleton side is the leaver candidate. This mapping is v0's, not a source's.

### 4.3 Team (n = 8–12)

Leonard's slow dynamics on the mean headings Ψ_k of informed subgroups 1, 2 and the uninformed subgroup 3, A_kl ∈ {0,1} the inter-subgroup couplings (L3873 [read]):

  dΨ_1/dt = sin(θ̂_1 − Ψ_1) + (K1/N)(A_12 N_2 sin(Ψ_2 − Ψ_1) + A_13 N_3 sin(Ψ_3 − Ψ_1)), and symmetrically.   (4.3a)

Choose-or-compromise threshold (L3874 [read], Eq. 7):

  decision manifolds stable iff cos θ̂_2 < 2r² − 1, i.e. θ̂_2 > θ_c = arccos(2r² − 1) = 2·arccos r.   (4.3b)

The identity is [derived] from cos 2u = 2cos²u − 1; check against the paper's own numbers: r = 0.9 → 51.7°, r = 0.6 → 106.3°, r = 0.707 → 90°. θ_c depends on the sensing range r and on nothing else: not on ω, not on K1, not on N_3. N enters only through K1/N (L3871). The uninformed-majority effect has one read dataset (golden shiners, LRT P = 0.018, 18 replicates; L3879 [read]); whether baboon splits test (4.3b) is an open contradiction (L3891, ⚠, not blocking a term).

No-split certificate (Wankhede, L4004 [read]), for the signed linear update ẋ_i = −w_i(x_i − p_i) + Σ_k a_ik(x_k − x_i) − x_i³/r_i:

  if w_i > Σ_{k ∈ enemies(i)} 2|a_ik| for every i, then μ_∞(J) < 0 and there is a unique globally exponentially stable equilibrium.   (4.3c)

Friends' terms cancel in the row sum, so (4.3c) is indifferent to a_ik ≠ a_ki. It forbids multistability, so it certifies **no** split only; it is sufficient, not a boundary. Structural precondition for "holds": κ(G) ≥ 2 (L3893 [read]; stability untested by its authors).

### 4.4 Crowd (n ≥ 100)

  r(t+1) = F[r(t)],  r(0) = share of instigators (θ_i = 0);  equilibrium F(r*) = r* at the first crossing from above.   (4.4a; L3960, L3961 [read])

Kuran's threshold, derived from two motives (L3965, L3967 [read]):

  V^i(y | x^i) = R(y) + N(y | x^i);  S = g(x(S^e)),  x(S^e) decreasing in the expected opposition share S^e.   (4.4b)

(4.4a) and (4.4b) are one object: a monotone map on a joined share, with fixed points. ⚠ **until D591**: the utility-term contradiction L3991 (089's reputational + integrity vs 066's intrinsic + expressive + reputational, open) sits under x(S^e); and the form is Kuran 1989's, Kuran 1991 being unread (089's method note). ⚠ **until D589**: monotone F assumes complements; Cantoni et al. report strategic substitutes (L3978 ⚠ sa) — if confirmed, F is not monotone and Theorem 6.3 fails. Comparative statics kept as read: a jump in S^e precipitates only if an alternative stable equilibrium exists; a leftward shift of x(S^e) can set the stage and precipitate; a shift of g alone cannot precipitate (L3968 [read]). Ordering of defection: those who privately prefer the regime most stay longest (L3970 [read]).

v0 reading of ω_T at crowd scale: Π_T = {joined, not joined} at r*; holds ⇔ r* ∈ {0, 1}; L_T = defectors by an instrument such as NAVCO sec_defect. The 3.5% regularity (16 of 18, post hoc; L3972 [read]) is not a term.

## 5. Composition

**How n enters.** Only through the composition operators, never as a property of a drive (091's conclusion, "n must enter as a parameter of the composition operators (share, correlation, memory, boundary)"; file text, no ledger line): K1/N in (4.3a); the enemy row sum in (4.3c), which grows with the number of enemies; the sampling variance of F over n draws (L3963 [read]); the weights w^i in Kuran's Y = Σ w^i y^i (L3966 [read]); and, for the certificate below, ρ(Γ) = (n − 1)g for uniform gain g on a complete graph, so the tolerable per-link gain falls as 1/(n − 1) ([derived] in 090 §3, one line from Corollary 7: the row sums of Γ are all (n − 1)g). What n does depends on what is held fixed (per-capita return, L4021 [read]).

**Composition law v0 DOES state (partition read off the relation layer):**

  Π_T = two-colouring(sgn A(T)) if A(T) is balanced (L4177); else {k : ω_1k > 0} ∪ {k : ω_1k < 0} (L3154); on the state layer, blocks = clusters of x at mutual distance ≥ 1 (L3825 [read], Krause dynamics, model only).   (5.1)

(5.1) composes across sizes in the trivial sense that it is defined for any n; it says who is in which block, not when, and the two rules can disagree (an unbalanced A has no two-colouring; a balanced A need not satisfy Marvel's three conditions).

**Composition law v0 DECLARES ABSENT (merge/split of drive state).** Given two blocks with drive states, v0 states no law for the drive state of their union, nor for how a block's state divides at a split. This is items 1, 2, 4, 5, 7 and 9 of 083's "composition laws that exist nowhere in what was read" (file §Inventory; no ledger line). The derived closure condition for merging two balanced groups (cross-tie signs = ε σ_i τ_j, L3807) is ⚠ because it rests on a restated theorem; it is a candidate, not a law.

**Certificate that composes across sizes.** Small-gain (L3996 [read]): if each member is ISS with gain matrix Γ (γ_ij the gain from j to i, γ_ii = 0, no relation assumed between γ_ij and γ_ji) and ρ(Γ) < 1 (linear gains; Γ∘D(s) ≱ s with slack in the nonlinear case), the interconnection is ISS. Two members: ρ(Γ) < 1 iff γ_12 γ_21 < 1. A one-way hierarchy has ρ = 0. **Limits:** sufficient only; identifies no equilibrium; forbids self-amplification by assumption, so it certifies **no-split / de-escalation only**; ρ(Γ) < 1 is strictly stronger than "every cycle gain < 1" (090 correction). Passivity is **not** used: it needs a balanced digraph (in-degree = out-degree, L4009 [read]; L3998 [read]), i.e. reciprocity, which v0 does not assume. No human γ_ij has ever been measured (090).

## 6. Candidate theorems

**6.1 (No-split certificate; theorem in continuous time, conjecture in turns).** Let x evolve by D1 + D2 with signed linear I_ij = a_ij(x_j − x_i), sign-stable A, and D3 absent or with a common goal. If w_i > Σ_{enemies} 2|a_ik| for all i, then there is a unique globally exponentially stable equilibrium and no state-layer partition with k ≥ 2 blocks is an attractor. *Hypotheses:* L4004 [read] (continuous time, with the cubic resource term). *Conjecture:* the Euler-turn version x(t+1) = x(t) + h f(x(t)) inherits it for h small enough that ‖I + hJ‖_∞ < 1; not yet shown.

**6.2 (Decoupling under closed gates; theorem).** If for all t and all pairs the incoming signal lies inside the gate (|x_j(t)| ∈ [K⁻_i, K⁺_i] at dyad scale; r(t) < θ_i at crowd scale), then (3.0) is D1 (+ D3) alone, the system is a product of independent scalar recursions, and x_i(t) → p_i monotonically at rate 1 − r_i. Π_T is then decided entirely by the relation-layer input A and L_T by the instrument; the dynamics add nothing. *Hypotheses:* L0853, L0857, L3919 [read]; |r_i| < 1. *Content:* this is what the Madhyastha/Steele threat (L3944, L3945 ⚠) would make the whole dyad law; D590 decides whether 6.2 is the special case or the rule.

**6.3 (Monotone cascade; theorem).** For F non-decreasing on [0,1] and r(0) = 0, the sequence r(t+1) = F[r(t)] is non-decreasing, converges to the least fixed point lfp(F), and for finite n does so in at most n turns; lfp(F) is not a function of any finite moment of F. *Hypotheses:* L3960, L3961 [read]; L1201, L1235 [derived, standard monotone-sequence argument and Knaster–Tarski]; L1225 [read]. *Corollary (⚠ until D591, D589):* with θ_i = x(S^e) from (4.4b), lfp exists for any g; it is lost if F is non-monotone (substitutes).

**Conjecture 6.4 (Merge closure).** Two balanced blocks with sign vectors σ, τ merge to a balanced union iff all cross-tie signs equal ε σ_i τ_j for one ε ∈ {±1} (L3807 ⚠). Two lines from (4.2b) once D588 lands; not a theorem of v0.

## 7. Refutation tests, one per size

- **n = 3.** In longitudinal signed triads, the one-negative triangle (two positive, one negative) must be under-represented relative to a sign-shuffled null and must convert to a balanced triangle faster than the all-negative triangle does. If conversion runs at the sign-shuffled null rate, the triad law (4.2b)/(5.1) is refuted. *Observation:* triangle counts and transition rates over time. *Data:* Newcomb fraternity (Doreian & Krackhardt 2001, D594, L4286 ⚠ sa); Askarisichani et al. 2019 (D594, L4284 ⚠ sa); static baselines from Epinions/Slashdot/Wikipedia (L4214, L4215, L4218 [read]).
- **Team.** A group with κ(G) ≥ 2 and every w_i > Σ_{enemies} 2|a_ik| must split less often than groups failing the condition; if it splits at the same rate, the certificate (4.3c) is refuted. If D2 adds no predictive gain over D1 for who leaves (the dyad finding at team scale), the coupling term goes. *Observation:* fission events against the certificate computed from measured ties and anchors. *Data:* Walker & Hill 2014 group fissions (D593, file 101); Couzin's shiners for (4.3b) (L3879 [read]); Mathieu 2015 for the lag direction (D592, L3911 ⚠).
- **Crowd.** If raising beliefs about others' turnout *lowers* participation (Cantoni: substitutes), monotone F is refuted.
