### 0.5 What the drive hypothesis survived

Stated plainly, because it decides what kind of thing v0.2 is.

1. **In nothing read does a drive fix the sign of a tie.** The six generators the map named (power → coalition, similarity → attraction, exchange → sentiment, shared desire → rivalry, status → attachment, threat → in/out) each supply a motive and none an equation from a drive level to a sign (112 §1, §8 and its verdict). What fixes the sign is structure: whether a shared object is excludable and the parties have parity and no common power (Hobbes, L4879 [read]; Sherif's functional-relations law, L4883 [read]; same-niche rivals −1 against complementary 0, L5428 [read]); the role of a strength gap — evaluation (a positive edge means "regards the target as higher", L5422 [read]), contest (the stronger extends the negative link, L4921 [read]) or a coalition market (the strongest is priced out by its own first offer, L4729 [read]); the legitimacy of the weights (earned inputs reverse the weak-pair result, L4758, L4760 [read]); the information regime (Byrne's d ≈ 7.6 with paper strangers, L5120 [read], against an odds ratio of 1.03 within a shared focus, L5138 [read]; contradiction L5171 open, a scope difference); and whether a threat has an identifiable rival (53% and 77% unfavourable after competition, L4935 [read]) or is universal (anti-immigrant sentiment fell and was unrelated to perceived threat, L5455 [read]). **The same drive state yields opposite signs across settings** (112 §2.2: five features, each with both signs in read sources).
2. **Person-level sender and receiver propensities carry most of the predictable sign variance, and no source gives them a mechanism.** The source's own majority sign scores 0.789/0.791 balanced accuracy on Epinions and Slashdot against 0.934/0.935 for the full model (recount, L4850 ⚠ sa; the paper's Figure 3 reads ≈ 0.83/0.81 by eye, L5472 open on the convention; the full model L4826 [read]); in speed dating, pre-meeting traits predict 4–18% of actor and 7–27% of partner variance and none of the dyad-specific part (L5463 [read]).
3. **Only the set-point term survives a change of era and sample** (116 item 11), and it is a baseline of expressed behaviour in one fifteen-minute task, not a measured drive (L4464, L4367–L4370 [read], one cohort of 1989–92 Seattle newlyweds per 059, before ≈ 1994 per 116; L5805 open on the years); spouses' set points correlate .60, which is selection or shared context (L5759 [read]); across individuals, mean affect carries the prediction and dynamics add little (L5764 ⚠ sa, not independent of the Sels samples).
4. **For this outcome the load-bearing content is structure, payoffs and composition** (116, "Net"): thresholds are "the net result of costs and benefits" with the origin of preferences excluded (L3959 [read]); Cantoni's sign comes from public-goods structure, crackdown probability and costly signalling (L5754 [read]); the group-size effect from the return to a contribution (L5779 [read]); community stability from turnover and size (L5740 [read]); fission from households (L4421 ⚠ sa).
5. **No sign dataset measures a drive level beside a sign**, so "drives as primitives" cannot be tested on existing data for this outcome (112 §8). What would: manipulate a need, appetite or threat with consent, with a pre-measured sign and the structure held fixed, and observe the sign change; none exists (112; 115's "what would count as a test" adds a person-level instrument, independent of the behaviour, predicting the response to a non-human member of known proportion).

**Where drives enter v0.2.** (a) The set point p_i of D1 (3.1), the one person-level term that survived. (b) Gates and thresholds: K⁻_i, K⁺_i at dyad scale; θ_i, θ̄_i and the crowd gate's sign operand σ (§4.4), whose inputs are costs and benefits that drives supply (Kuran's integrity and reputational terms, L3965 [read]; fear changing sides, L4390 [read]); the admission and label gates of §5.5. (c) Costs inside gates: arrest cost (L3959), crackdown probability rising with size (L4348 [read]), repression as a threshold shift (L3976 [read]). (d) The propensities a_i, b_j of the sign law (§4.5, R-E) as person-level parameters fitted per population and period — the place a mechanism would go if one is ever found. (e) The motive each registry law is named for (reward-seeking and affect regulation, L4962 [read]; the appetite for an unshareable thing, L5409 [read]; control-seeking, L5407 ⚠ sa; fear and security, L5412 ⚠ sa): the name of the law, not a term in it. Nowhere in v0.2 does a drive level appear as a variable that sets a sign or a coupling.

**Open decision, Aldred's (not a recommendation).** Keep "holds together or splits, and who leaves" as the first outcome — where, on this evidence, drives are gate inputs and priors and structure does the work — or switch to an outcome where drives are the obvious variables: conformity and compliance at membership scale (the Asch and Milgram re-runs of 113 §5 are its data, 33% wrong answers in Bern 2021 against 36.8% in the 1950s, L5548 [read], with a rate that has run from 1 of 396 trials to Asch's level across populations, L5553 ⚠ sa), or effort and efficiency at group scale (108's exchange chain; 115's facilitation results). v0.2 takes neither; everything below is written for the first, and the structure (sorts, gates, composition, validity record) transfers to either.

### 0.6 The validity record (adopted from 116 §5)

Every term, theorem and ledger line v0.2 rests on carries: `class` (S structural, F form, M magnitude, I instrument; a **sign flag** where a source shows the sign changing with a moderator); `era` ([start, end] of data collection, `theory`, or `unknown` with a bound); `medium`; `population`, `n_units`, `unit`; `size_range`; `scale`; `independence` (samples, a shared cohort counted once); `evidence_type`; `sample_status` (in-sample, cross-validated, preregistered, replicated); `retest` ∈ {none-found, same-data, new-data-consistent, new-data-inconsistent, mixed} and `retest_data_after_2015`; `sign_stable` with moderators and flip points; `reflexive`; `instrument`; `review_by`; `v02_status` ∈ {adopted-as-form, prior-with-interval, hypothesis, quarantined, setting-operand}. The populated record for v0.2's terms is §9.2.

Rules (every threshold an editable setting with slack; the defaults marked [ours] are 116's proposals adopted here as defaults, not findings):
1. **S lines are exempt from decay** and carry a `hypotheses` list; each hypothesis is its own M or I record. A theorem whose every hypothesis is `none-found` is true and empty for prediction — the state of 6.1 and of every `models/01` theorem today: no gain matrix or enemy weight has been measured anywhere (L5736, L5743; 116 §4; the only measured influence weights are nonnegative, L4450 [read]).
2. **M lines with `retest` ∈ {none-found, same-data} or `sample_status` = in-sample enter only as a prior with a stated interval**, never as a point value in a prediction; outputs that depend on them carry "domain: prior".
3. `review_by` default = data end + 15 years for face-to-face laboratory magnitudes, + 5 for platform- or feed-mediated ones, + 3 after a rule becomes public [ours]: the spans over which 116 saw material change (a decade for campaign success, L5771 [read]; a quarter for a firm's balance in a crash, L5791 [read]; two years for a platform magnitude, L5627 open).
4. **Domain test.** A law is in domain for a use iff the medium matches, the unit size lies within `size_range` widened by a factor 1 (S), 2 (F) or 1.5 (M) [ours], and the population differs from the evidence's only on factors the source says do not matter; otherwise the algebra returns "out of domain", not a number.
5. **`sign_stable` = no forces a setting operand** in every equation that uses the term, with its moderators in the record: the crowd gate's σ (since v0.1); the group-size effect's return and n-range (L5779 [read]; L5817 settled: the large-group advantage holds at a return of 0.30 only); community size for the turnover effect (L5740 [read]); the site for the +−− triad cell (L5796 [read]).
6. Independence counts by sample (the Gottman cohort is ten lines and one sample; Sels 2020's Study 2 is Sels 2016's couples, L5763). 7. Instrument-bound results carry their instrument; a use with another instrument is out of domain until a bridge result exists (none bridges absence-as-negative, L5746 [read], to a distrust vote, L5748 [read], or either to a rank recode, L4476 [read]). 8. `era` is data years; a paper's year is `pub_year` and is never used for age (116 item 2: 25 of v0.1's 46 empirical lines cite papers from 2014 on, and 19 of those rest on data from 2009 or earlier or undated).

Settings from 113 (proposed there; Aldred's call, 113 §9.4): **S1** a shrinkage multiplier ×0.5, slack 0.25–0.75, on any magnitude whose only source is one small published significant study (replication-to-original ratios 0.49, L5513 [read]; 0.25, L5516 [read]; ≈ 0.5 overall and 0.71 for true positives, L5518 [read]; a significance filter alone gives 0.71 at 50% power, L5497 [derived in 113 §2.6, not shown here]) — v0.2 carries it as a flag with the default shown and applies it to no term; **S2** an `unrefreshed` flag rather than an age discount, because no read source re-estimates a coupling weight, gate, inertia or tie sign across eras (113 §9.2; for the sign model L4857) — adopted; **S5** crowd-scale time constants carry an era and medium index (a top-50 hashtag's residence fell from 17.5 h to 11.9 h between 2013 and 2016, L5561 [read]) — adopted for λ in (S2) of §4.5 and for any cascade duration or lag; **S6** no cohort variable unless a source identifies one (age = period − cohort, L5496 [read]) — adopted.

## 1. The outcome

**Definition 1.1 (outcome).** A group G is a finite set of n persons. Fix a horizon T ∈ ℕ (turns, §2). The outcome at T is the pair

  ω_T = (Π_T, L_T),  L_T ⊆ G,  Π_T a partition of G ∖ L_T into non-empty blocks B_1, …, B_k.

L_T is the **leaver set**; a block is a set of members still together at T. holds(ω_T) ⇔ k = 1 and L_T = ∅; splits(ω_T) ⇔ k ≥ 2; who-leaves(ω_T) = L_T with the block map β_T : G ∖ L_T → {1, …, k}. holds and splits are not complementary: k = 1 with L_T ≠ ∅ is attrition without schism. v0.2 is written against ω_T, not against any one instrument.

## 3. The three drives the evidence forces

Generic form (discrete turns): x_i(t+1) = x_i(t) + Δ¹_i(t) + Δ²_i(t) + Δ³_i(t).   (3.0)

**D1 — set point.**  Δ¹_i = −(1 − r_i)(x_i − p_i), with r_i = r_i^− if x_i < p_i and r_i^+ otherwise.   (3.1)

Derivation from Gottman's printed form [derived, shown]: a_i + r_i x_i = x_i − (1 − r_i)(x_i − p_i) with p_i = a_i/(1 − r_i) (L0853, L2096 [read]). The same form is Wankhede's −w_i(z_i − p_i) (L3993), Altafini's diagonal with p_i = 0 (L3856), the FJ anchor (L1025), 081's fill–discharge cell dx/dt = r(1 − x) − s x = −(r + s)(x − r/(r + s)) (L1824 [derived], one line), the quadratic member of the Keramati–Gutkin family at n/m = 2 (L2022 [read]), and — in a designed agent — the S++ aspiration update (§5.5, L5698 [read]). The two-regime r_i is one full-text fit of one cohort (L4368 [read]; **hypothesis**, §9.2). At crowd scale D1 is Kuran's integrity term N(1 − |x^i − y^i|) (L3965 [read]) and 1991's internal payoff rising with x^i (L4393 [read, WP]). Evidence that D1 carries most of the signal: the uninfluenced steady states are the strongest in-sample divorce correlates of ten parameters, F(1,123) = 14.86 and 18.40 (L4464 [read]; **prior with interval**: in-sample, ten tests, no correction, no cross-validation found, 116 §3.1), and the refit of the same cohort puts the partner term near zero (L4367, §6.2). What survives a change of era and sample is the level, not the dynamics (§0.5).

**D2 — gated affiliation.**  Δ²_i = Σ_{j ≠ i} I_ij(x_j, x_i), I_ij = 0 inside i's gate.   (3.2)

Ledger-backed forms by size:
- dyad: the O-jive — 0 for K⁻_i ≤ x_j ≤ K⁺_i, bounded constants beyond; a function of the partner's level, not of the difference (L0857 [read]). ## 6. Candidate theorems

**6.1 (No-split certificate; theorem in continuous time AND in Euler turns for the linear law).** Let x evolve by D1 + D2 with the difference-form coupling I_ij = a_ij(x_j − x_i), sign-stable A (λ = 0 in (S2)), D3 absent or with a common goal, and let E_i = Σ_{k ∈ enemies(i)} |a_ik|, F_i = Σ_{k ∈ friends(i)} a_ik. If w_i > 2E_i for all i, there is a unique globally exponentially stable equilibrium; so no **second** attractor exists and a split cannot be a matter of history. The equilibrium itself may be sign-clustered: the certificate forbids multistable splitting, not block structure. *Continuous time:* L4004 [read] (with the cubic term). *Turns [derived, shown]:* for the linear law f(x) = −W(x − p) + (coupling), J_ii = −w_i − F_i + E_i, J_ik = a_ik (k ≠ i), and the Euler map x(t+1) = x(t) + h f(x(t)) has linear part M = I + hJ. Take h with h·max_i(w_i + F_i + E_i) < 1. Then M_ii = 1 − h(w_i + F_i − E_i) > 0, and row i of |M| sums to 1 − h(w_i + F_i − E_i) + h(F_i + E_i) = 1 − h(w_i − 2E_i). **6.3 (Monotone cascade; theorem).** Let F : [0,1] → [0,1] be non-decreasing and r(0) = 0, r(t+1) = F[r(t)]. *(i) Finite n [derived, shown]:* F takes values in {0, 1/n, …, 1}; by induction r(1) = F(0) ≥ r(0) and r(t) ≤ r(t+1) ⇒ F(r(t)) ≤ F(r(t+1)), so the sequence is non-decreasing on a finite set and is constant from some r* = F(r*) on, reached in ≤ n steps; if s is any fixed point, r(t) ≤ s ⇒ r(t+1) ≤ F(s) = s, so r* = lfp(F). *(ii) General F:* the bounded non-decreasing sequence converges to r* = sup_t r(t), F(r*) ≥ r*, and F(r*) = r* if F is left-continuous. (L1201, L1235 [derived]; Knaster–Tarski on the subset lattice.) *(iii)* lfp(F) is not determined by the mean and variance of F (L1225 [read]), nor by any summary of the parts' equilibria and sizes under merge (Claim 1.6). *Lemma 6.3a [derived, shown]:* with y_i = 1 iff r ≥ θ_i(r), F(r) = n⁻¹#{i : r − θ_i(r) ≥ 0}; if r ↦ r − θ_i(r) is non-decreasing for every i (θ_i constant or falling with r, Kuran's T(S), L4395), F is non-decreasing. *Corollary (conditional):* with θ_i = x(S^e) from (4.4b) and ∂θ_i/∂r ≤ 1 for all i, lfp exists for any g. Cantoni's sign (L4355) needs members with ∂θ_i/∂r > 1 at that margin, equivalently an upper gate θ̄_i < 1 crossed there; then F = F_θ − F̃_θ̄ need not be monotone and the iteration may cycle (`sim/tests/test_granovetter_crowds.py`). 6.3 applies on [0, r*] whenever F̃_θ̄ = 0 there; the manufactured-consensus shift (§5.5) preserves monotonicity and raises lfp.

**6.4 (Merge closure; theorem, [derived, shown] from L4327).** Let G_1, G_2 be balanced and each connected, with two-colourings σ, τ, and add cross ties with signs s_ij. The union is balanced iff there is one ε ∈ {±1} with s_ij = ε σ_i τ_j for every cross tie. *Proof.* (⇐) x = (σ, ετ) satisfies sign = x_i x_j on every line. (⇒) a balanced union has a labelling x with sign = x_i x_j on every line; on a connected balanced block the labelling is unique up to a global flip (if x, x' both work then x x' is constant across every line, hence on the component), so x|_1 = ε_1σ, x|_2 = ε_2τ and s_ij = ε_1ε_2 σ_iτ_j. ∎ For disconnected blocks replace "one ε" by one sign per pair of components.
