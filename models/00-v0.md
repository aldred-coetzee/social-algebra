**Aim, one line.** One outcome in two layers, at n = 2–3, 8–12 and 100+, from the fewest drives the evidence forces, every term credited and carrying a validity record (§0.6), a stated refutation per size, before any population model. **Layer A, first: activity** — how much the group does and whether it runs away. **Layer B, second, produced:** whether the group holds together or splits, and who leaves, read off Layer A's state and the sign registry, never input.

**The decision and why (Aldred, 2026-11-01; the note).** After the hinge finding (batch 4: in nothing read do drives fix the sign of a tie, §0.5), the battery, and both views put to him, Aldred chose activity first, with holds/splits kept as a second layer the model *produces* from the sign registry rather than a first outcome the model is handed. The reasons, as recorded: the only two group states with proved composition laws are drive-side (the no-escalation certificate on the gain matrix, `models/01`; the threshold distribution with the sandwich and ignition, `models/02` Claim 1); the battery found the sign registry's form to be a person effect with a residue of a different form, the one directed sign law reducing to reception, and weak balance the best single rule — an exogenous or person-level sign; the activity-side laws got real tests in a modern medium and partly held (co-movement with the recent majority on Wikipedia elections, a one-sided lower gate on participation; on Apache, growth against collapse rather than mean activity). At three sizes the outcome reads: escalation in a dyad, effort and sustained activity in a team, turnout in a crowd. Costs carried: the Newcomb result becomes a second-layer result (§5); activity needs per-member participation data, which is scarce (117, L4665); the crowd law is identifiable from aggregate curves only up to the stall, so interventions are needed above it (§4.4, §8).

## 1. The outcome, two layers

**Definition 1.1 (Layer A: activity).** A group G is a finite set of n persons; fix a horizon T ∈ ℕ (turns, §2). Each member carries a non-negative **activity** a_i(t) ≥ 0 per turn, the group's activity is A(t) = Σ_i a_i(t) (or its per-member mean, by instrument), and the Layer A outcome is the pair

  α_T = (A(·)|_{0..T}, runaway_T),  runaway_T ∈ {0, 1},

the activity path and whether the group *ran away*. **"Runs away" is the failure of the certificate at that size** (§7): the dynamics have an attractor other than the one the members' set points fix, and the path has entered it — a second attractor at dyad scale, unbounded (or saturated-high) growth under positive coupling at team scale, a full cascade at crowd scale. ## 3. The three drives, restated for activity

Generic form: x_i(t+1) = x_i(t) + Δ¹ + Δ² + Δ³ (3.0), applied to the activity scalar a_i where the instrument makes a_i the state (team, crowd) and to x_i where a_i is read off it (dyad).

**D1 — set point.** Δ¹_i = −(1 − r_i)(x_i − p_i) (3.1), r_i state-dependent in one fit (L4368; hypothesis). Derivation from Gottman's a_i + r_i x_i [derived, v0.2.1 §3]; the same form in Wankhede (L3993), Altafini (L3856), FJ (L1025), the fill–discharge cell (L1824 [derived]), Keramati–Gutkin at n/m = 2 (L2022), S++ (L5698). **For activity it is persistence toward one's own rate**: on Apache a member's next-month activity from her own history alone has AUC 0.90 [battery: apache §5.5]; at dyad scale the uninfluenced steady states are the strongest in-sample divorce correlates (L4464 [read]; prior with interval). D1 carries the signal at both sizes where it has been measured.

**D2 — gated coupling.** Δ²_i = Σ_{j≠i} I_ij(x_j, x_i), 0 inside i's gate (3.2). Ledger forms: the O-jive at dyad scale (L0857; K⁻ quarantined); Leonard's similarity-opened a_jl at team scale (L3871, L3872); the gate as the whole term at crowd scale (L3959, L1831; Kuran L3965); two signed-linear forms agreeing iff a_ij ≥ 0 [derived, one line]. **Where the battery bears, exactly:**
- *Dyad gate, dead zone.* On Bitcoin OTC/Alpha 2010–16 the rating a trader gives back, as a function of the **single raw rating received**, has no flat band: +2 against +1 is +0.85 (chi² 221) on OTC, +1.06 on Alpha; flatness over +1..+4 rejected at p < 1e-10; the literal O-jive (bounded constants beyond free thresholds) loses out of sample to a graded two-sided form with the band closed by 0.46 squared points (6.5 SE, OTC; 3.1 SE Alpha); the best dead-zone fit's band is the one rating +1, a knee not a zone [battery: gate_reciprocity_bitcoin Verdict, Review]. **What is refuted is a flat band covering +1 and +2 in the single received rating** (and on Alpha only bands reaching +2; its {−1, +1} is untested, 17 responses). **(S3)'s dead zone on the smoothed state z̄ is UNTESTED, not refuted** — the Bitcoin test reaches z̄ only through one received rating. The sign gate there is large: a negative received rating moves the response by **−8.36 against +1 pooling all negatives** (67% of them −10); the mildest, −1 against +1, is **−4.1** (p = 1e-07) on OTC; 95% of responses to a negative are negative against 2.5% after a positive. **72% of responses are exact copies** of the rating received (83% of those within ten minutes), so most pairs are two views of one deal, and the slope is an association net of persons, not an isolated influence (shared-view confound: a linear one is excluded by equal increments, a non-linear one undecided). **Admitted form in that medium:** I(x) graded in the partner's level with a sign jump and a saturation knee near +7, dead-zone widths as soft settings starting at 0. The couples' K⁻ (L0834) is neither supported nor refuted by it (another medium, §0.6 rule 7); the linear partner term ≈ 0 of L4367 does **not** carry over (0.83 per point here): two media, two coupling constants.
- *Crowd gate, sign.* On Wikipedia elections 2005–13, **σ = + as short-memory co-movement**: the share of the **previous 10 votes** enters P(support) with logit coefficient **1.44** against a within-election shuffle null of −0.01, **z +16.1** (every year from 2005, every size class; the same for the previous 5 and 20, and in an election-fixed-effect model); a net negative association (substitutes as co-movement) is refuted at this scale [battery: crowd_gate §2, Review]. **Influence against shared shock is undecided**: a shared-shock burst gives the same z and the same time symmetry (dependence on the next 10 votes 1.63 against the previous 10's 1.44; planted forward-influence data look the same), and the usual time-reversal placebo cannot discriminate. The **cumulative** share — the law's state variable r(t) — carries little (spline excess +1.25 against the window's +3.27) and its coefficient is **negative at 40 or more earlier votes** (−1.26, −5.47), **set aside as non-stationarity** (elections open with a run of supports and drift), not read as substitutes. **L = 10 was chosen in-sample** (hypothesis, §0.6). The Hong Kong substitutes (2SLS −0.47, −0.65; L5768 [read]) are not repeated here, and L4363's "the sign flips with turnout" stays open. σ stays a **setting operand**.
- *Crowd gate, participation.* A **one-sided lower gate** is supported: arrivals fall below the composition-and-clock null when the tally runs against the candidate, already in the first 24 h (bands < 50% and 50–70%: −0.21 and −0.09 log-points), and are flat at and above 85%; **"stalls below 70%" is a hard-coded band edge, not an estimated breakpoint**. **No path-dependent upper gate** at decisive success (bands ≥ 85% at or above their null); a gate acting through the final composition is absorbed by the control and untested. No state-triggered ignition (the contested band's late rise is below composition, z −2.7), **but the .85–.95 band rises more in the last 12 h than the shuffle gives (1.24 against 1.13, z +5.8), uninvestigated**. The closure attaches to failure and rises as final support falls (397 of 402 elections silent after 72 h failed) and is partly institutional [battery: crowd_gate §3.3–3.5].
- *Team coupling.* Mean-field: the others' activity share adds +0.0007 AUC to a member's own history on Apache [battery: apache §5.5]; the **tie-based** D2 (who replies to whom) has not been run. The partner-term-near-zero finding of the dyad (L4367; 6.2) reappears at team scale on that proxy only.

**D3 — goal attraction.** Δ³_i = ω_i sin(g_i − x_i), linear ω_i(g_i − x_i) (3.3; L3864, L3866 [read]; trained fish L5731; hypothesis). Unchanged; Leonard's switch (4.3a, b) is where it acts.

## 4. Update laws per size, for activity

### 4.1 Dyad

  x_1(t+1) = a_1 + r_1 x_1(t) + I_12(x_2(t)), symmetrically for 2 (4.1; L0853, L0857 [read]; lag 1 taken), a_i(t) = [x_i(t)]⁻ read off.

**Runaway** = a second attractor of (4.1) reached from x(0). **At the 6-s lag in the one cohort there is none to be had from the linear coupling**: the partner coefficients are 0.007 [−0.020, 0.035] and 0.018 [−0.007, 0.044] against own inertia 0.60/0.26 (L4367, L4368 [read]); two smartphone samples agree (L4375, L5762, L5763 [read]); so (4.1) is, by 6.2, a product of independent recursions with one fixed point each, and the dyad's Layer A outcome reduces to **the negativity level set by p_1, p_2** plus whatever the **quarantined gate** K⁻ would add. Validity: 1989–92 Seattle newlyweds (L5805 open), face-to-face, fifteen minutes; no independent replication of any dyad equation found (116). The daily-scale abstracts disagree (L1806, L1409, L4379, L5766, L5767 ⚠ sa). **In a public rating medium the coupling is large and graded** (§3, Bitcoin): 0.83 per received point with a sign jump — the dyad law has at least two media-bound forms, and v0.3 carries both with their media.

(ii) If ρ(M) ≥ 1 and M is irreducible, then for every p ≥ 0 with some p_i > 0 (one member has a positive rate; irreducibility makes every member reachable from her) a(t) is unbounded: **the only runaway under non-negative coupling is unbounded growth; there is no second attractor and no bistability.** *Proof.* (i) ρ(M) < 1 gives (I − M)^{-1} = Σ_k M^k ≥ 0 (`models/01` F3), so the affine map has the unique fixed point a* and a(t) − a* = M^t(a(0) − a*) → 0; a* − p = (I − M)^{-1}[(I − R) − (I − M)]p = (I − M)^{-1}Γp ≥ 0; monotonicity in Γ because every M^k is. (ii) Let v > 0 be a left Perron vector of M, vᵀM = ρvᵀ (Perron–Frobenius, M irreducible). Then vᵀa(t) = ρ^t vᵀa(0) + Σ_{k<t} ρ^k vᵀ(I − R)p ≥ t · vᵀ(I − R)p → ∞ since ρ ≥ 1 and vᵀ(I − R)p > 0. ∎ *Reading.* The certificate is `models/01`'s with gain matrix M, so 2.1, 3.1–3.4 hold for activity verbatim (inertia enters the diagonal; (F1)–(F3) need only M ≥ 0): a fragment of a certified team is certified; a merger runs away only through the two-way loop; the exact size law for uniform teams reads ρ_i = r + (n_i − 1)g_i. With a saturating response φ(a_j) in place of a_j the ρ(M) ≥ 1 regime is a high-activity second attractor rather than unbounded growth — stated, not proved here. *Hypothesis record:* empty (no γ, r or p measured for a team; Apache gives own-history persistence only, §4.3). **This is the form in which "runs away" is the certificate's failure at team scale (1.1).**

**7.6 (Reactive member joins a team; corollary of 7.5 and `models/01` 3.2, new).** Let a team be certified, ρ(M) < 1, and admit a member p with inertia r_p ∈ [0,1), column c_{·p} ≥ 0 (what members' activity gains from p) and row c_{p·} ≥ 0 (what p's activity gains from them). The enlarged team is certified **iff** c_{p·}(I − M)^{-1}c_{·p} < 1 − r_p. *Proof.* Take G_2 = {p} with Γ_2 = [r_p]: (I − Γ_2)^{-1} = 1/(1 − r_p); P = (I − M)^{-1}c_{·p}c_{p·}/(1 − r_p) is rank one with ρ(P) = c_{p·}(I − M)^{-1}c_{·p}/(1 − r_p); apply 3.2. ∎ *Uniform case [derived, one line]:* n members with gain g and inertia r, ρ = r + (n − 1)g, (I − M)^{-1}1 = 1/(1 − ρ)·1; a member who answers every member with gain c and is answered with gain d is safe iff **c d n < (1 − ρ)(1 − r_p)**. *Reading.* A broadcast-only agent (c_{p·} = 0) is always safe; a reactive LLM member that replies to everything (c_{p·} large) and is replied to (c_{·p} large) has a tolerable loop gain falling as 1/n and shrinking with its own persistence r_p — the one place a non-human's D1 (S++, L5698) enters a certificate. Hypotheses: no gain measured for any non-human member (115 §12).

**7.7 (One-sided lower gate reads F only to the stall; proposition, new; Proposition B of the identifiability file restated for the law as now written).** Let the gate be one-sided (θ̄_i = 1 for all i), so F is non-decreasing and the orbit from r(0) is non-decreasing to r* = lfp (7.3). Then any F′ non-decreasing with F′ = F on the orbit points {r(0), …, r*} produces the same orbit, and F′ may differ from F arbitrarily on (r*, 1]. *Proof.* The orbit is determined by the values of F at its own points, by induction; monotonicity of F′ is preserved by any choice above r* that stays ≥ F′(r*) = r*. ∎ *Consequence:* under the gate the battery supports, the activity state of a crowd is observable from its own path only up to where it stopped; a seeded run from r(0)′ > r* reads F on its own orbit above r*; ignition of a merge (Claim 1.4) depends on F_1 at the host's stall M, which the intruder's orbit never visits unless τ_1 ≥ M. Exact; no hypothesis beyond the gate's form.

## 8. Refutation tests per size, for activity

Each names a dataset, the rule to write first, and what the identifiability result says it can and cannot fit.

- **Dyad — a graded-response test on an affect series, with the smoothed-state dead zone actually tested.** *Data:* any dyadic expressed-affect series with a lag — the Gottman SPAFF series are not public; the Sels smartphone samples (L5762, L5763 [read]) are the authors'; a public candidate is a dyadic-conversation corpus with continuous valence annotation per few seconds (e.g. K-EmoCon, 16 dyads, Zenodo — **named from memory; DOI, licence and fit to be checked before use, not cited here**). *Rule:* compute z̄_ij(t) by (S2) for a grid of λ (and λ = 1, the raw partner level); fit I(z̄) in the forms the Bitcoin test used — null, linear, one-sided, two-sided graded with the band closed (T), two-sided with a free band (D), saturated — out of sample with thresholds re-chosen inside folds; flatness Wald tests over every band of two or more observed bins. *Refuted:* the dead zone, if D does not beat T out of sample and flatness is rejected over every band, at every λ; *supported:* if a band of width > 0 is flat at some λ and D beats T. *What this can fit:* within the Gottman form the set points, inertias and I(·) are identifiable from one series (the one cohort did it); it cannot separate influence from a shared stimulus without an exogenous input to one partner.
