## 1. The outcome

**Definition 1.1 (outcome).** A group G is a finite set of n persons. Fix a horizon T ∈ ℕ (turns, §2). The outcome at T is the pair

  ω_T = (Π_T, L_T),  L_T ⊆ G,  Π_T a partition of G ∖ L_T into non-empty blocks B_1, …, B_k.

L_T is the **leaver set**; a block is a set of members still together at T. holds(ω_T) ⇔ k = 1 and L_T = ∅; splits(ω_T) ⇔ k ≥ 2; who-leaves(ω_T) = L_T with the block map β_T : G ∖ L_T → {1, …, k}. holds and splits are not complementary: k = 1 with L_T ≠ ∅ is attrition without schism. v0.1 is written against ω_T, not against any one instrument.

**Instruments that realise ω_T** (092 §2; none spans all three sizes as read):

|---|---|---|---|

## 3. The three drives the evidence forces

Generic form (discrete turns): x_i(t+1) = x_i(t) + Δ¹_i(t) + Δ²_i(t) + Δ³_i(t).   (3.0)

**D1 — set point.**  Δ¹_i = −(1 − r_i)(x_i − p_i), with r_i = r_i^− if x_i < p_i and r_i^+ otherwise.   (3.1)

Derivation from Gottman's printed form [derived, shown]: a_i + r_i x_i = x_i − (1 − r_i)(x_i − p_i) with p_i = a_i/(1 − r_i) (L0853, L2096 [read]). The same form is Wankhede's −w_i(z_i − p_i) (L3993), Altafini's diagonal with p_i = 0 (L3856), the FJ anchor (L1025), 081's fill–discharge cell dx/dt = r(1 − x) − s x = −(r + s)(x − r/(r + s)) (L1824 [derived], one line), and in one dimension the quadratic member of the Keramati–Gutkin family D = |p − x|^{n/m} at n/m = 2 (e.g. n = 4, m = 2; the ledger's Euclidean case n = m = 2 is linear in distance), i.e. gradient descent on a quadratic drive (L2022 [read]). The two-regime r_i is the one full-text fit (L4368 [read]). At crowd scale D1 is Kuran's integrity term N(1 − |x^i − y^i|) (L3965 [read]) and 1991's internal payoff rising with x^i (L4393 [read, WP]). Evidence that D1 carries most of the signal: the uninfluenced steady states are the strongest in-sample divorce correlates of the ten parameters tested, F(1,123) = 14.86 and 18.40 (L4464 [read]), and the refit of the same data puts the partner term near zero (L4367, §6.2).

**D2 — gated affiliation.**  Δ²_i = Σ_{j ≠ i} I_ij(x_j, x_i), I_ij = 0 inside i's gate.   (3.2)

Ledger-backed forms by size:
- dyad: the O-jive — 0 for K⁻_i ≤ x_j ≤ K⁺_i, bounded constants beyond; a function of the partner's level, not of the difference (L0857 [read]). The husband's K⁻ is the one coupling parameter with a direction in the divorce data (−7.71 divorced vs −5.36 stable, F(1,123) = 8.25; L0834 [read]; one of ten tests). The **linear** partner term, fitted with set point and inertia in one model, is ≈ 0 (L4367 [read]; §6.2).
- team: I_ij = (K1/N) a_ij sin(x_j − x_i), unsigned a_ij that opens or closes with heading similarity: dη_jl/dt = K2(ρ_jl − r), a_jl = 1/(1 + e^{−η_jl}) (L3871, L3872 [read]); K1 the ratio of social coupling to goal strength (L3865).
- signed linear, **two forms**: the difference form I_ij = a_ij(x_j − x_i) (Wankhede, L3993/L4004) and Altafini's a_ij x_j − |a_ij| x_i (L4304 [read]), which agree iff a_ij ≥ 0 [derived, one line: a_ij x_j − |a_ij| x_i = a_ij(x_j − x_i) ⇔ a_ij = |a_ij|]. For a_ij < 0 the first repels i from x_j, the second pulls i toward −x_j. v0.1 uses the difference form in (4.3c) and 6.1 and Altafini's in the §4.2 state layer, and says which each time.
- crowd: the gate is the whole term: y_i(t+1) = 1 iff θ_i ≤ r(t) ≤ θ̄_i (L3959, L1831 [read]; θ̄_i from §4.4); Kuran's reputational utility R with f, F increasing in the shares (L3271, read carrier L3957; L3965 [read]).

Objective coupling is not D2: 047's D¹ = D_a + w_1 D_b (L2908 [derived]) couples objectives, and in the quadratic case its gradient in x_i has no cross term. (v0 cited L0992 here; that line is a value decomposition V_i = D^i + (γ − 1)S_i, not a coupling — audit item B; dropped.)

**Empirical referent for D1 and D2 together (D595) [derived, shown].** FJ's map x(k+1) = AWx(k) + (I − A)x(0), A = diag(1 − w_ii), W row-stochastic from the chips (L4447, L4450, L4451 [read]). Row i, using Σ_j w_ij = 1: Σ_j w_ij x_j = x_i + Σ_{j≠i} w_ij(x_j − x_i), so

  x_i(k+1) − x_i(k) = −w_ii (x_i − x_i(0)) + Σ_{j≠i} (1 − w_ii) w_ij (x_j − x_i),

i.e. (3.0) with D1 at p_i = x_i(0), 1 − r_i = w_ii, and D2 in the difference form with a_ij = (1 − w_ii) w_ij ≥ 0, Σ_{j≠i} a_ij = (1 − w_ii)². With the read mean self-weight w_ii = 0.643 (se 0.016; 30 tetrads, L4452): 1 − r_i ≈ 0.64 and Σ_j a_ij ≈ 0.13 per FJ iteration — D1 outweighs D2 by about five to one. Caveats: the identity rests on FJ's convention a_ii = 1 − w_ii (an assumption, L4451); the time unit is one model iteration, not a turn of known length (settlement 3–27 min, L4456); weights are self-reported after the outcome, nonnegative, n = 4, student strangers; the per-pair distribution of w_ij is in the unread chapter 4 (L4458 ⚠ sa); Bruggeman's 0.3–0.5 is not an FJ value (L4460). Numerically checked.

**D3 — goal attraction.**  Δ³_i = ω_i sin(g_i − x_i) on S¹; linear case ω_i(g_i − x_i).   (3.3)

Leonard's preference term (L3864 [read]); Couzin's |ω| as intransigence (L3866). On a line D3 and D1 have one form; they are kept separate because g_i is a stated option and p_i a measured baseline, and because θ_c (4.3b) depends on the angle between goals and on nothing in D1.

**Not forced.**
- *Status.* No read law bearing on ω_T has a status term. Leskovec's status rule (L4196 [read]) predicts the sign of a directed tie, a rival to balance for the sign input, not a state term. FJ's closure rising with influence centrality (L4455) is a dynamic of the weights, not a state term.
- *Threat.* A **cost inside a gate** only: arrest cost in the threshold (L3959); repression as a shift of Kuran's threshold (L3976); crackdown probability rising with believed size (L4348 [read]); Kuran's fear changing sides (L4390 [read, WP]). No read law has threat as a state with its own dynamics.
- *A triad-consistency drive.* L3796 ⚠ sa; the product rule is a constraint on the relation graph, true whatever the drives are — L4290 [derived, shown]: the sign of a closed walk is Π_{(i,j)∈C} sgn a_ij, a functional on the Z₂ cycle space of the relation graph taking no argument from x, p, r or g, so it is invariant under every state law; under a gauge x ↦ Dx, D = diag(σ), edge signs change by σ_iσ_j and every cycle product is unchanged because each node of a cycle appears in exactly two of its edges. Askarisichani's balanced states being absorbing and conversion slow (L4441) is consistent with a weak consistency pressure and equally with sparsity (L4429); D&K's non-uniform movement (L4445 ⚠ sa) warns against a single such drive.

**Why this is smaller than 050's basis.** 050 §4 proposes affiliation, status, threat, seeking, care, mating, anger as "a modelling judgement" (L4470 [derived]); the Kenrick seven (self-protection, disease avoidance, affiliation, status, mate acquisition, mate retention, kin care; L3523 ⚠ sa) is one input list to it, not the basis (audit item B). v0.1 keeps only drives that appear as a term in a read law bearing on ω_T: D2 is 050's affiliation, D3 the goal part of seeking, D1 has no counterpart. v0.1 is a floor.

### 4.2 Triad (n = 3)

Relation layer, continuous time as read (discrete version: Antal's CTD, L3828 [read], simulation only):

  dA/dt = A², da_ij/dt = Σ_k a_ik a_kj   (4.2a; L0898 [read])

with factions S = {k : ω_1k > 0}, T = {k : ω_1k < 0} for the leading eigenvector when λ_1 > 0, λ_1 ≠ λ_2, all ω_1k ≠ 0 (L3154 [read]); a cartoon, and the Zachary fit used an offset (L3826).

Static constraint:

  sign(C) = Π_{(i,j)∈C} sgn(a_ij); balanced iff every cycle (semicycle, if directed) is positive iff G two-colours with positive lines within and negative across   (4.2b; L4170, L4171, L4177 [read, two readers: 084, 096])

**Scope (⚠ lifted).** C&H state (4.2b) for an s-graph "containing any number of points" with no completeness hypothesis: "complete" and "triangle" occur nowhere in the definition or theorem, their worked graphs are not complete, and their Fig. 10 is 3-balanced but not 4-balanced (096 §1.3; L4337 settles L4298). The triangle form is a corollary on complete graphs (L4328; brute-force checked n = 4–6 in 096). The proof is in Harary 1953, **not read** (L4342 open); v0.1 carries it [derived, shown]: (⇐) along a cycle Π x_i x_j telescopes to +1; (⇒) root each component, set x_v = sign of a root–v path, well defined because two such paths close to a walk whose edge multiset is a union of cycles, all positive; then sign(uv) = x_u x_v. On K3 only b ∈ {0, 1} occurs: Heider's rule is the n = 3 case.

**What stays ⚠: the strict (−)(−) cell as an empirical rule (L4299 open, both halves).** Leskovec's learned-coefficient test (WWW) has the (−)(−) path failing to predict a positive edge in all three networks, while the CHI triangle-count test has enemy-of-my-enemy over-represented in two of three; the two tests are unreconciled. The data support Davis's weak form — forbid only the one-negative triangle (L4202, L4203, L4214, L4218 [read]; L4185 ⚠ sa, Davis unread by any file, L4340) — so v0.1 keeps (−)(−) undefined, not +, which is 084's recommendation L4293 (cited as such). Askarisichani agrees in kind: the all-absent triad holds 0.75 of the stationary mass and is stable, the one-negative 0.01 (L4439 [read]), under absence-as-negative (L4429).

**State layer (⚠ lifted; term changed to carry its hypotheses).** ẋ = −L[A]x, L_jj = Σ_m |a_jm|, L_jk = −a_jk, i.e. Altafini's form of D2 (L3849; L4304 [read at source]). **Dichotomy (L4307, L4308 [read, manuscript]):** for a connected undirected signed graph (Thm 1), or a strongly connected, digon-sign-symmetric digraph (a_ij a_ji ≥ 0; Thm 2), the flow admits bipartite consensus — |x_i| → α > 0, signs by the two-colouring, limit (1/n)1ᵀDx(0)·D1 in the undirected case — iff A is balanced; if A is unbalanced, x(t) → 0 for every x(0). General static digraph (L4310 [read]): x → 0 iff the graph is neither balanced nor contains an in-isolated balanced subgraph; bipartite consensus iff balanced and quasi-strongly connected. **Counterexample outside the hypotheses (L4311 [read]):** a_12 = a_21 = −1, a_31 a_32 > 0, 1 and 2 ignoring 3: quasi-strongly connected, unbalanced, and x → (ξ, −ξ, ρξ), ρ = (a_31 − a_32)/(|a_31| + |a_32|), ξ = (x_1(0) − x_2(0))/2 (x_1 − x_2 is conserved, x_1 + x_2 → 0; numerically checked) — neither polarisation nor zero. So on a complete symmetric triad: 0 or 2 negative ties → polarisation; 1 or 3 → every state to 0; one tie absent → vacuously balanced → polarisation; a mutually hostile pair ignoring the third → the equilibrium line. Caveats, not ⚠: the manuscript, not the IEEE typeset text, was read; the dichotomy is the w_i = 0, p_i = 0 case — with set points the state-layer result is (4.3c), and 095 reads the zero limit as an artefact of fixed signs and no stubbornness; L3835/L3837/L3840 are settled in the notes of L4317/L4319, whose status fields still say open.

v0.1 reading of ω_T at n = 3: Π_T is the two-colouring of sgn A(T) where balanced (L4177 [read]; strict (−)(−) cell ⚠ L4299); where it is not, the X² flow picks the side by sgn ω_1 (L3154); a member whose both ties are negative and who is on the singleton side is the leaver candidate. This mapping is v0's, not a source's.

### 4.3 Team (n = 8–12)

Leonard's slow dynamics on the mean headings Ψ_k of informed subgroups 1, 2 and uninformed 3 (L3873 [read]):

  dΨ_1/dt = sin(θ̂_1 − Ψ_1) + (K1/N)(A_12 N_2 sin(Ψ_2 − Ψ_1) + A_13 N_3 sin(Ψ_3 − Ψ_1)), and symmetrically.   (4.3a)

Choose-or-compromise threshold (L3874 [read], Eq. 7): decision manifolds stable iff cos θ̂_2 < 2r² − 1, i.e. θ̂_2 > θ_c = 2·arccos r   (4.3b)

The identity is [derived] from cos 2u = 2cos²u − 1; checks: r = 0.9 → 51.7°, 0.6 → 106.3°, 0.707 → 90°. θ_c depends on the sensing range r only. N enters only through K1/N. One read dataset (golden shiners, LRT P = 0.018, 18 replicates; L2347, read carrier L3879); whether baboon splits test (4.3b) is open (L3891 ⚠, blocking nothing).

No-split certificate (Wankhede, L4004 [read]), difference-form coupling, ẋ_i = −w_i(x_i − p_i) + Σ_k a_ik(x_k − x_i) − x_i³/r_i:

  if w_i > Σ_{k ∈ enemies(i)} 2|a_ik| for every i, then μ_∞(J) < 0 and there is a unique globally exponentially stable equilibrium.   (4.3c)

## 6. Candidate theorems

**6.1 (No-split certificate; theorem in continuous time AND in Euler turns for the linear law).** Let x evolve by D1 + D2 with the difference-form coupling I_ij = a_ij(x_j − x_i), sign-stable A, D3 absent or with a common goal, and let E_i = Σ_{k ∈ enemies(i)} |a_ik|, F_i = Σ_{k ∈ friends(i)} a_ik. If w_i > 2E_i for all i, there is a unique globally exponentially stable equilibrium; so no **second** attractor exists and a split cannot be a matter of history. The equilibrium itself may be sign-clustered: the certificate forbids multistable splitting, not block structure (audit item D). *Continuous time:* L4004 [read] (with the cubic term). *Turns [derived, shown]:* for the linear law f(x) = −W(x − p) + (coupling), J_ii = −w_i − F_i + E_i, J_ik = a_ik (k ≠ i), and the Euler map x(t+1) = x(t) + h f(x(t)) has linear part M = I + hJ. Take h with h·max_i(w_i + F_i + E_i) < 1. Then M_ii = 1 − h(w_i + F_i − E_i) > 0 (since w_i + F_i − E_i ≤ w_i + F_i + E_i < 1/h and w_i + F_i − E_i > E_i + F_i ≥ 0), and row i of |M| sums to 1 − h(w_i + F_i − E_i) + h(F_i + E_i) = 1 − h(w_i − 2E_i). So ‖M‖_∞ = max_i (1 − h(w_i − 2E_i)) < 1 iff w_i > 2E_i for all i; the affine map is a contraction in ‖·‖_∞, has one fixed point (Banach), which solves f(x*) = 0 and so is the continuous-time equilibrium, and convergence is geometric at rate ‖M‖_∞. (The same row sums give μ_∞(J) = −min_i(w_i − 2E_i) < 0, the continuous condition.) ∎ *Reproduced numerically:* `sim/tests/test_wankhede_certificate.py` (unique equilibrium, the ‖I + hJ‖_∞ formula, and a hostile-pair counterexample with two attractors when w_i < 2E_i).

**6.2 (Decoupling under closed gates; theorem — and at dyad scale the rule for linear coupling).** If for all t and all pairs the incoming signal lies inside the gate (x_j(t) ∈ [K⁻_i, K⁺_i] at dyad scale; r(t) ∉ [θ_i, θ̄_i] at crowd scale), then (3.0) is D1 (+ D3) alone, a product of independent scalar recursions. With D3 absent, x_i(t) → p_i monotonically at rate 1 − r_i. With D3 present (linear case), x_i(t+1) = x_i − (1 − r_i)(x_i − p_i) + ω_i(g_i − x_i), so x_i → ((1 − r_i)p_i + ω_i g_i)/(1 − r_i + ω_i), the weighted mean of set point and goal, at rate 1 − r_i + ω_i (monotone iff that rate ≤ 1) — v0.1 had written "→ p_i" for both cases; corrected 2026-10-31 after sim/test_v01_update.py exposed it [derived, one line]. In either case Π_T is decided entirely by the relation-layer input A and L_T by the instrument. *Hypotheses:* L0853, L0857, L2096 [read]; |r_i| < 1. *Content after D590:* the one full-text refit of the Gottman newlywed data with set point, state-dependent inertia and a linear partner term in one model (129 couples, lag 6 s; L4365 [read]) gives mean partner coefficients 0.007 [−0.020, 0.035] (husband) and 0.018 [−0.007, 0.044] (wife) against own inertia 0.60–0.61 below the set point and 0.26–0.29 above (L4367, L4368); per-couple intervals exclude zero for 10.1% (husband→wife) and 6.2% (wife→husband) of couples against 5% by chance, after shrinkage (L4369). The bound is on the coefficient of x_j(t−1) whatever is subtracted from it, so it transfers to a_ij in the signed-linear form: |a_ij| ≤ 0.044 against 1 − r_i ∈ [0.39, 0.74]. At this lag and form 6.2 is the rule. Untested by it: the O-jive gate (L0857), whose K⁻ carries the one divorce direction (L0834); lags above 6 s (Sels: 36% of 50 couples with any significant partner slope at ≈ 1.2 h, chance ≈ 34%, L4375 [read]); the daily-scale abstracts disagree (L1409, L4379 ⚠ sa).

**6.3 (Monotone cascade; theorem).** Let F : [0,1] → [0,1] be non-decreasing and r(0) = 0, r(t+1) = F[r(t)]. *(i) Finite n [derived, shown]:* F takes values in {0, 1/n, …, 1}, so r(t) does too; by induction r(1) = F(0) ≥ 0 = r(0) and r(t) ≤ r(t+1) ⇒ F(r(t)) ≤ F(r(t+1)), so the sequence is non-decreasing on a finite set, hence eventually constant at some r* with F(r*) = r*; while not yet constant each step rises by ≥ 1/n, so r* is reached in ≤ n steps, with no continuity assumption. If s is any fixed point, r(t) ≤ s ⇒ r(t+1) = F(r(t)) ≤ F(s) = s, so r* = lfp(F). *(ii) General F [derived, shown]:* the non-decreasing bounded sequence converges to r* = sup_t r(t); F(r*) ≥ F(r(t)) = r(t+1) for all t gives F(r*) ≥ r*, and if F is left-continuous F(r*) = lim F(r(t)) = r*; the lfp argument is as in (i). (This is L1201 [derived]; L1235's cascade operator is the same statement on the subset lattice: Y ↦ {i : |Y|/n ≥ θ_i} is monotone, so Knaster–Tarski gives a least fixed point, reached from ∅ in ≤ n steps — L1235's work field says derived, so it is counted derived.) *(iii)* lfp(F) is not determined by the mean and variance of F (L1225 [read], Granovetter's pair as witness). *Lemma 6.3a [derived, shown]:* with the gate y_i = 1 iff r ≥ θ_i(r), F(r) = n⁻¹#{i : r − θ_i(r) ≥ 0}; if r ↦ r − θ_i(r) is non-decreasing for every i (true when θ_i is constant or falls with r — Kuran's T(S), L4395), each indicator is non-decreasing and so is F. *Corollary (conditional; the ⚠ of v0 is replaced by the hypothesis):* with θ_i = x(S^e) from (4.4b) and ∂θ_i/∂r ≤ 1 for all i, lfp exists for any g. Cantoni's sign (L4355) needs members with ∂θ_i/∂r > 1 at that margin, equivalently an upper gate θ̄_i < 1 crossed there; then F = F_θ − F̃_θ̄ need not be monotone, and for finite n a non-monotone step map may have no fixed point and the iteration may cycle. 6.3 applies on [0, r*] whenever F̃_θ̄ = 0 there.

**6.4 (Merge closure; theorem, [derived, shown] from L4327 — was Conjecture 6.4 on L3807 ⚠).** Let G_1, G_2 be balanced and each connected, with two-colourings σ, τ (sign(ij) = σ_iσ_j on G_1, τ_iτ_j on G_2), and add cross ties with signs s_ij. The union is balanced iff there is one ε ∈ {±1} with s_ij = ε σ_i τ_j for every cross tie. *Proof.* (⇐) x = (σ, ετ) satisfies sign = x_i x_j on every line, so the union is balanced by (4.2b). (⇒) A balanced union has a labelling x with sign = x_i x_j on every line. On a connected balanced block the labelling is unique up to a global flip: if x, x' both work then x_i x'_i = x_j x'_j across every line, so x x' is constant on the component. Hence x|_1 = ε_1σ, x|_2 = ε_2τ and s_ij = x_i x_j = ε_1ε_2 σ_iτ_j. ∎ For disconnected blocks replace "one ε" by one sign per pair of components. L3807 stays `sa` in the ledger (own derivation); its content is now a theorem with the derivation shown.
