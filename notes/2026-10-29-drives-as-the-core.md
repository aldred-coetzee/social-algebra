# Drives as the core of the algebra

_2026-10-29_

Aldred, after the first synthesis: *"I think we need to go a lot deeper and
do more research. The idea is that we end up with an algebra which expresses
basic human drives and group drives, which we can compose into equations,
which we can then use to start making theorems and use to control or predict
behavior. So I was thinking things like, curiosity, fear, desire, etc."*

What this changes:

- The aim is sharper than "operators and operands": the primitives are
  **drives**, of persons and of groups. Acts between people matter as the
  way drives are triggered, expressed and transmitted.
- **It reopens Q4** (`notes/2026-10-29-gating-decisions.md`). That answer
  put affect and scalars in a later layer; drives are internal, graded state
  and are now central. Whether a drive level is discrete (none/low/high) or
  a real number is open again, and is Aldred's call once the drive survey is
  in.
- The target is theorems and prediction or control, so the algebra needs
  laws (how drives combine, conflict, transmit), not only a vocabulary.
  The first survey's warning still applies: individual-level prediction of
  behaviour has a poor record [docs/prior-work/07, MusicLab and Fragile
  Families]; aggregate and reachability claims have done better.

The `models/00-sorts-and-state.md` draft was stopped before it landed.

Drive survey started 2026-10-29, five files: 15 taxonomies of drives and
motives (the candidate primitives), 16 formal models of motivation (where
drives are already in equations), 17 curiosity, fear, desire and their
interactions, 18 group drives and collective emotion, 19 computational
agents with drives.
