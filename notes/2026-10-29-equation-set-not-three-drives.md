# The goal is an equation set, not three drives

_2026-10-29_

Aldred: *"Do not limit to just those three, they were just examples; idea is
we formulate a new or updated equation set."*

Curiosity, fear and desire were illustrations. The target is:

- **A full drive inventory** for persons and for groups, not a chosen few.
  The candidate core comes from the cross-walk in `docs/prior-work/15`
  (belonging, status, fear/self-protection, curiosity/seeking, autonomy and
  competence, care, anger, mating) widened by `41` (self-conscious, moral
  and relational drives: shame, guilt, pride, envy, gratitude, trust,
  revenge) and `42` (dynamics drive by drive: status, belonging, care, play,
  sex, mastery, fairness, meaning, aggression, disgust).
- **An equation set**: either new, or an update of existing equations. The
  survey has turned up many partial ones to start from: homeostatic drive
  and reward (Keramati & Gutkin), Atkinson–Birch dynamics of action,
  Bischof's Zurich model, Gottman–Murray and Liebovitch coupling, Spinoza's
  and Herbart's derivations, Gul–Pesendorfer temptation, Battigalli–
  Dufwenberg belief-dependent motives, honeybee cross-inhibition, network
  games, mean-field games, and the composition and stability machinery of
  `40`. Each equation adopted is credited to its originators.

So the drive synthesis must end in a **candidate equation set** with every
term traced to a validated source (or marked as ours), not a narrative.
