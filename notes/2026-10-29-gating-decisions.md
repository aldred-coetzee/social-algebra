# Gating decisions: operands, state, dynamics

_2026-10-29_

Aldred answered the three questions in `docs/synthesis-brief.md` that decide
the formal home, taking the coordinator's default each time:

- **Q1 Operands:** persons, roles and groups only. Classes and institutions
  come in later as kinds of group. Out for now: events as operands
  (Luhmann), institutions as their own sort.
- **Q4 State:** discrete and relational first (who holds which role in
  which group, who is committed to whom). Scalars (affect, opinion, power)
  are a later layer on top. Out for now: ACT-style affect arithmetic,
  opinion dynamics.
- **Q5 Dynamics:** discrete turns, nondeterministic (an act may have several
  outcomes). Claims take the form "state Y is reachable from state X by
  these acts". Rates and probabilities come later, for fitting data. Out for
  now: continuous time, statistical prediction.

**Why these defaults:** each is the cheapest to extend later. A scalar
layer can be added over a relational state, probabilities can be put on a
nondeterministic relation, and new kinds of group can be added, but none of
these can easily be taken back out once the laws depend on them.

Still open (held until now, in the synthesis's list): what a role is, dyad
or triad as the atom, whether the other's view of me is state, which acts,
observability, fit-first or write-first. Choosing a home follows from these.
