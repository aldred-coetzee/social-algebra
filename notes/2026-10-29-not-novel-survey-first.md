# Not novel — survey first

_2026-10-29_

Aldred, at the first working exchange: *"No this is not novel, research what
has already been done in this field and store and validate and revert."*

The opening move is therefore not to invent a notation but to find out which
algebras of social structure and interaction already exist, what each takes
as operands and operators, and where they stop. The survey lives in
`docs/prior-work/`, one file per tradition, each checked by a second pass
that confirms citations exist and that the claims about them are accurate.

Whatever social-algebra becomes should be stated relative to that survey:
which existing algebra it extends, combines or departs from, and why.
