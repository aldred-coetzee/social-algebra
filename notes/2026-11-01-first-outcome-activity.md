# First outcome: activity

_2026-11-01_

After the hinge finding (batch 4: drives fix no tie sign in anything read),
the test battery, and both views (next-pattern's and this session's) put to
him, Aldred chose: **activity first** — how much the group does and whether
it runs away — with holds/splits kept as a second layer the model *produces*
from the sign registry rather than a first outcome the model is handed.

Why, in brief: the only two group states with proved composition laws are
drive-side (the no-escalation certificate on the gain matrix; the threshold
distribution with the sandwich and ignition); the battery found the sign
registry's form to be a person effect with a residue of a different form,
the one directed sign law reducing to reception, and weak balance the best
single rule — an exogenous or person-level sign; the activity-side laws got
real tests in a modern medium and partly held (co-movement with the recent
majority on Wikipedia elections, a one-sided lower gate on participation;
on Apache, growth versus collapse rather than mean activity). At three
sizes the outcome reads: escalation in a dyad, effort and sustained
activity in a team, turnout in a crowd.

Costs to carry: the Newcomb result becomes a second-layer result; activity
needs per-member participation data, which is scarce (117); the crowd law is
identifiable from aggregate curves only up to the stall, so interventions
(seeded runs) are needed above it (identifiability test).

v0.3 is written on this basis.
